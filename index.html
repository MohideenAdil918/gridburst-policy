✏️ 1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to GridBurst: Doodle Notebook Puzzle ("GridBurst", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Mohideen Adil.

Your privacy is paramount to us. GridBurst is designed as an offline-first, lightweight casual block puzzle game. You can enjoy the full puzzle experience without ever creating an account or providing sensitive personal information.

💡 Key Privacy Principle: We do not sell your personal data. We do not track you across unrelated third-party apps. Any data handled is strictly used to deliver gameplay, ensure fair high-score verification, and display advertisements through trusted industry partners (Google AdMob).

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Last Updated: October 26, 2026

📋 2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Optional Google Sign-In: If you choose to link your Google Account for cross-device cloud sync and global leaderboards, we receive your public display name, email address, and avatar URL via Google Credential Manager.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: If you email our support team, we receive your email address and any feedback or bug descriptions you choose to submit.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

GridBurst stores all core game data locally inside your device's internal application storage:

  • Current board matrix, high scores, and combo streaks.
  • Inventory counts of power-up tools (Eraser, Rotate, Bomb, Refresh).
  • Sound, vibration, theme settings, and VIP/ad-free unlocked state.

C. Information Automatically Processed

Data Category Examples Purpose
Device & OS Identifiers Device manufacturer, model, Android OS version, display resolution. Ensure 120 FPS hardware rendering, correct screen scaling, and bug diagnosis.
Advertising ID (GAID) Google Advertising ID (can be reset in Android Settings). Deliver rewarded video ads, banners, and app-open ads via Google AdMob.
Anti-Cheat Telemetry Total game moves, final score hash, run elapsed duration. Validated by our serverless Cloudflare Edge Worker to prevent fraudulent leaderboard scores.

🧩 3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To keep GridBurst free for everyone, we partner with industry-standard third-party providers. These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies:

Google AdMob (Mobile Ads)

GridBurst displays banner ads, interstitial transitions, app-open ads, and voluntary rewarded video ads (e.g. to earn game tools or revive a lost match) via Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic crash data to serve contextually relevant ads and prevent click fraud.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset Advertising ID.
  • Learn more: Google Ads Policy ↗ Google Privacy Policy ↗

Google Play In-App Billing

Optional purchases (such as the Permanent VIP Pass or tool packs) are processed directly by the Google Play Store. We never receive or store your credit card, bank details, or billing addresses.

Cloudflare Edge Infrastructure

Our lightweight high-score validation and anonymous cloud backup run on Cloudflare Workers and D1 Database. Data is processed securely in transit using modern TLS 1.3 encryption.

🧸 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

GridBurst is a casual, family-friendly doodle puzzle. Protecting children’s privacy is extremely important to us:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • GridBurst ad configurations honor Google Play’s Families Policy and COPPA regulations. Where applicable, ads are requested with neutral, non-personalized configurations.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at adildrags@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete the data from our servers.

🗑️ 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Google Play Policy Compliance: You have the unconditional right to request the deletion of all data associated with your game account at any time.

Because GridBurst does not require account creation, most data is stored exclusively on your own phone. Here is how you can erase everything:

  • On Your Device: Simply uninstall the app or go to Android Settings > Apps > GridBurst > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This immediately erases all scores, offline saves, and settings.
  • Cloud Leaderboards & Synced Profiles: To delete any synced leaderboard scores, Google-linked cloud profiles, or support records from our database, send an email to adildrags@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My GridBurst Data". We process all deletion requests within 48 hours.

⚖️ 6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following privacy rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: The right to request what personal data (if any) we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to Be Forgotten": The right to demand that your data be permanently removed.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never degrade your gameplay, limit levels, or charge different prices for exercising your privacy rights.
  • California Privacy (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information as defined under California law.

🔒 7. Data Security & Policy Updates

We employ industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for any network requests between the game and our verification servers. While no system can guarantee 100% invulnerability, we minimize risk by simply not collecting unnecessary sensitive information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game enhancements or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
